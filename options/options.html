05 / ON YOUR TERMS

Settings

Simple controls. Your discipline stays in your hands.

Emergency access

To bypass a gate, open Haki’s popup on that trading tab and hold Emergency Access for five seconds. Bypass applies to that page only.

This control targets the active trading tab. In a settings tab, use the popup on your platform instead.

Private by design

Your rules and preferences stay in this browser. Haki has no account, analytics, or server. It does not collect your trades, credentials, account balances, positions, or browsing history.
