Privacy policy · September 27, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Stored in your browser

Haki stores your trading rules, selected website hostnames, preferences, and confirmation date and time in chrome.storage.local. It does not save which individual rules you checked. When damaged settings are repaired or settings are reset, one recovery copy may also be kept locally.

No collection or transmission

Haki does not collect brokerage credentials, account numbers, balances, positions, orders, trade history, profit/loss data, passwords, or browsing history. It does not send your rules or preferences to a server. There is no authentication, analytics, advertising SDK, or remote logging.

Website access

Haki asks for access to each HTTPS website you enable. It uses the current hostname to decide whether to show your rules and inserts its own overlay. While the gate is active, it consumes pointer and keyboard events to prevent interaction with the platform. Those events are not recorded or transmitted. Haki does not read trading data from the page.

Your controls

You can edit or reset settings, remove custom sites, revoke browser site access, pause protection, or uninstall the extension. Reset keeps one local recovery copy; uninstalling clears the extension’s local storage. Chrome profiles, device backups and browser management policies are outside Haki’s control.
