PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your rules stay yours.

Privacy policy · September 27, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Stored locally

Haki stores your rules and their ordering, selected website hostnames, protection and onboarding preferences, reminder frequency, optional new-tab and example-ad preferences, and your last confirmation time and local date using chrome.storage.local. It never persists individual checked-rule state. Emergency access is temporary.

When damaged settings are repaired or settings are reset, one recovery copy may be kept locally. Reset keeps that copy; uninstalling clears extension storage.

No collection or transmission

Haki does not collect your trades, account numbers, balances, brokerage credentials, positions, orders, trade history, profit/loss data, passwords, or browsing history. It does not read trading data from the platform’s DOM or send your rules to a server. There is no backend, authentication, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or remote logging in the extension.

Website permissions

Haki asks for access to each exact HTTPS host you enable. Its broad optional host declaration allows user-chosen sites; Haki never requests blanket browsing access. It uses the current hostname to decide when to display its own overlay. While active, the overlay consumes keyboard and pointer events to prevent interaction with the platform. Those events are not recorded or transmitted, and the guards are removed when the gate closes.

Removing a custom website releases its optional permission. You can also revoke site access in Chrome, pause Haki, reset it, or uninstall it. Chrome profile management, device backups, other extensions, and organizational policies are outside Haki’s control.

New tabs and example ads

The optional new-tab view displays your local rules. When disabled, Haki opens Chrome’s built-in New Tab page; Chrome’s own network behavior is governed by Chrome’s policies. The example ad cards are clearly labeled local mockups with fictional sponsors. There is no ad network, external destination, profiling, or tracking. Any future real advertising or data collection would require updated policies and controls before release.

This website

The checked-in Haki landing page has no forms, payment processor, cookies, or analytics code. Hosting providers may process standard request information such as IP addresses to serve the website and download; hosting-specific policies should be supplied by the operator when the site is deployed.
